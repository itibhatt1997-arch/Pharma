About Starus Pharma

ISO 9001:2015 certified · Headquartered in Hyderabad, India

Starus Pharma is an ISO 9001:2015 certified pharmaceutical group headquartered in Hyderabad, India, focused on delivering innovative and meaningful healthcare solutions.

Since its inception, the organization has focused on identifying evolving healthcare needs and developing a strong presence in selected therapeutic areas.

Our philosophy is built around scientific knowledge, quality, innovation, customer relationships and responsible business practices.

We seek to create long-term value for patients, healthcare professionals, business partners, employees and other stakeholders by continuously strengthening our capabilities and expanding our healthcare portfolio.

Starus Division

Chronic & Specialized Therapies

The growing prevalence of chronic diseases has created an increasing need for effective, accessible and innovative healthcare solutions.

Conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, arthritis, gastrointestinal disorders, neurological disorders and psychiatric conditions continue to place significant demands on healthcare systems and communities.

Recognizing these evolving healthcare requirements, Starus Pharma established its focused Starus Division to address selected chronic and specialized therapy areas.

With focused leadership and a commitment to scientific and market knowledge, Starus Division continues to strengthen its capabilities and contribute to the evolving healthcare landscape.

The division is committed to:

  • Developing and introducing differentiated healthcare solutions.
  • Strengthening its presence across key chronic therapy segments.
  • Working closely with physicians and healthcare professionals.
  • Building scientific and therapeutic expertise.
  • Continuously identifying opportunities for innovation.
  • Supporting healthcare professionals with reliable and meaningful therapeutic solutions.

Corporate Social Responsibility

Our Commitment to a Sustainable Future

At Starus Pharma, we recognize the importance of maintaining a balance between economic development, social progress and environmental sustainability.

Our CSR philosophy focuses on creating sustainable and measurable social impact through initiatives in:

CSR Governance & Implementation

Our CSR initiatives operate within the Company's Board-approved CSR framework and applicable regulatory requirements. A dedicated operating team supports the implementation and monitoring of CSR programs under the guidance of the CSR Committee.

Our approach includes:

  • Project planning and evaluation
  • Defined objectives and impact assessment
  • Periodic monitoring and reporting
  • Continuous evaluation of project progress
  • Review of outcomes and social impact
  • Transparent allocation and utilization of CSR resources

Sustainable projects and programs form the foundation of our CSR activities, with their scope and budgets reviewed periodically based on their relevance, impact and organizational priorities.

Any surplus arising from CSR projects or activities is managed in accordance with applicable laws and does not form part of the Company's business profits.

Our Leadership

Our Leadership

Dr. P. Uday Kiran

Leader, Starus Pharma

Dr. P. Uday Kiran brings extensive experience in pharmaceutical sales, marketing, business development and healthcare markets.

He holds a Doctorate in Business Administration, along with a Postgraduate qualification in Biotechnology and an M.Phil. in Molecular Biology.

With a strong combination of scientific knowledge and commercial expertise, Dr. Uday Kiran has developed a deep understanding of pharmaceutical markets, healthcare professionals and evolving therapeutic requirements.

Before establishing Starus Pharma, he worked with Sun Pharma as a Manager, where he gained valuable experience in the pharmaceutical industry and was recognized with performance-based awards.

As the leader of Starus Pharma, Dr. Uday Kiran continues to provide strategic direction across the Group, with a focus on:

  • Healthcare innovation
  • Scientific and market-driven decision-making
  • Sustainable business growth
  • Strong physician and healthcare-professional relationships
  • Product and therapeutic development
  • Organizational excellence
  • Responsible corporate citizenship

His vision is to build Starus Pharma into a trusted and respected healthcare organization that creates long-term value for patients, healthcare professionals, employees, partners and society.
